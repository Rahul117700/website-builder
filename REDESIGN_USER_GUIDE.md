## 📑 The 4 Tabs Explained

### 1. 🎨 **Design Tab** - Make It Look Great
Where you customize the visual appearance of your funnel.

**What you can do:**
- ✅ Choose color scheme (6 beautiful presets or custom colors)
- ✅ Pick primary & secondary colors
- ✅ Set button color
- ✅ Upload a cover image (PNG, JPG, GIF)

**Pro Tips:**
- Use contrasting colors for better readability
- High-quality cover images = more professional look
- Button color should stand out from background

---

### 2. 📝 **Content Tab** - Write Your Message
Add the text that sells your product.

**What you can do:**
- ✅ Write main headline (max 100 characters)
- ✅ Add subheadline for more context (max 200 characters)
- ✅ Set CTA button text (e.g., "UNLOCK FULL VIDEO", "Get Started")

**Examples:**
```
Headline: "Master the Digital Product Game"
Subheadline: "3-video outline + sales copy PDF. 
For selling short video tutorials"
Button: "UNLOCK FULL VIDEO"
```

**Pro Tips:**
- Headline should grab attention
- Subheadline explains the benefit
- CTA should be action-oriented

---

### 3. 👤 **Seller Tab** - Build Trust
Show your customers who they're buying from.

**What you can do:**
- ✅ Your name (required)
- ✅ Email address (required for support)
- ✅ Phone number (optional)
- ✅ Website URL (optional)
- ✅ Bio/About you (optional, max 300 characters)

**Pro Tips:**
- Use your real name - builds trust!
- Clear email helps customer communication
- Bio humanizes your brand

---

### 4. 📦 **Product Tab** - Upload & Sell!
This is where you define your product.

**What you can do:**
- ✅ Product name (e.g., "Video Tutorial Package")
- ✅ Description (explain what's included)
- ✅ Price in INR (₹)
- ✅ Product type (Software, PDF, Video, Course, etc.)
- ✅ **Upload product file** ⭐ **NEW FEATURE!**

### 📤 How to Upload Your Product File

#### Step 1: Fill in Product Basics
Before uploading, make sure you've entered:
- ✅ Product Name
- ✅ Price

#### Step 2: Scroll to "Product File"
Look for the section with upload area and upload icon

#### Step 3: Upload Your File
- **Click** on the upload area OR
- **Drag & drop** your file directly

**Accepted file types:**
- 📄 PDF documents
- 🎥 MP4 videos
- 💻 ZIP files
- 🖼️ Images (JPG, PNG)
- 📊 Code files & more

**File size limit:** 100MB

#### Step 4: Wait for Upload
A progress indicator shows while uploading

#### Step 5: Done! 
Green checkmark shows file is uploaded ✓

#### Changing Your Product File?
Just click "Remove" and upload a new one

## 🔄 The Workflow

### First Time Setup
```
1. Design Tab → Pick colors & upload cover image
   ↓

   ↓

   ↓
```

## 💡 Pro Tips & Tricks

### Color Selection
- **Choose from presets** for instant professional look
- **Custom colors** for brand matching
- Test contrast for readability
- Look good on preview before saving

### Content Writing
- **Headline:** 50-100 characters is ideal
- **Subheadline:** Explain the core benefit
- **Button:** Use power words like "Unlock", "Start", "Get Access"
- Preview updates in real-time!

### Product Upload
- **Test your file** works properly before uploading
- **Zip your files** if uploading multiple items
- **Name files clearly** for easy identification
- Can replace file anytime

### Preview Modes
- 📱 **Mobile:** See how it looks on phones
- 💻 **Tablet:** Check tablet responsiveness
- 🖥️ **Desktop:** Full width desktop view
- Use eye icon to hide/show preview panel

## ⚠️ Important Notes

### Required Fields for Publishing
```
✅ Headline (in Content tab)
✅ Product Name (in Product tab)
✅ Product Price (in Product tab)
✅ Product File (in Product tab)
✅ Your Name (in Seller tab)
✅ Email Address (in Seller tab)
```

### Validation Rules
- Product file can't exceed 100MB
- Product name and price needed before file upload
- Headline can't be empty
- Email must be valid format

### Auto-Saves
- Customizations are not auto-saved
- **Must click [💾 Save Changes]** to persist
- Preview updates in real-time (doesn't need saving)

## 🎯 Common Tasks

### How to Change Colors?
1. Click **Design** tab
2. Select preset or choose custom colors
3. See live update in preview
4. Click **Save Changes**

### How to Upload Cover Image?
1. Click **Design** tab
2. Click upload area under "Cover Image"
3. Select PNG, JPG, or GIF (max 5MB)
4. Click **Save Changes**

### How to Upload Product?
1. Fill **Product Tab** basics (name, price)
2. Scroll to "Product File"
3. Click or drag file to upload
4. Wait for success message
5. Click **Save Changes**

### How to Publish?
1. Complete all required fields
2. Click **[🚀 Publish]** button
3. Funnel goes live!
4. Share your funnel URL with customers
