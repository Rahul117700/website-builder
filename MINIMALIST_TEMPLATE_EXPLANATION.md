## 🚀 How to Fill In Your Channel

### Step 1: Add Basic Info
Click the **"🏠 Basic"** tab on the left:
1. **Channel Name**: "tesy" ✅ (already set)
2. **Description**: Add a longer description like:
   - "World's best design video for your portfolio"
   - "Creative designs and tutorials"
   - "Professional design services"

### Step 2: Upload Images
In the **"🏠 Basic"** tab:
1. **Profile Image**: Upload a profile picture (will show in navigation)
2. **Cover Image**: Upload a banner/header image (will show in About section)

### Step 3: Add Welcome Message
In the **"🏠 Basic"** tab:
1. Scroll to **"Welcome Message"**
2. Write a warm welcome message for visitors
3. This will create a full "About" section

### Step 4: Add Products/Content
Click the **"🛍️ Products"** tab:
1. Click **"Add Your First Product"**
2. Add your portfolio items, courses, or digital products
3. Upload product images
4. These will appear in the portfolio grid
