What is a Subscription-Based Channel?

A subscription-based channel is your own branded online space where you can share exclusive content with paying subscribers. Think of it as a combination of YouTube and Patreon—you get the flexibility of a content platform with the monetization power of a subscription service.

Key Features:

  • Your Own Branded Channel: Customize colors, images, and layout to match your brand
  • Multiple Content Types: Share videos, PDFs, courses, software, templates, code, and more
  • Flexible Monetization: Choose between subscription access or individual product sales
  • Professional Templates: Start with beautiful, ready-made templates
  • Easy Content Management: Upload, organize, and manage all your content in one place
  • Secure Payments: Integrated payment processing for seamless transactions

Why Create a Subscription Channel?

1. Recurring Revenue Stream

Unlike one-time sales, subscriptions provide predictable monthly income. Once you build a subscriber base, you have a steady revenue stream that grows over time.

2. Build a Community

Subscribers are more engaged than one-time buyers. They're invested in your content and more likely to provide feedback, share your channel, and become long-term supporters.

3. Share Unlimited Content

With a subscription model, you can share as much content as you want. Upload videos, PDFs, courses, software—everything your subscribers need in one place.

4. Professional Branding

Create a channel that looks professional and reflects your expertise. Customize every aspect to match your brand identity.

5. Flexible Pricing

Set your own subscription price. Whether it's ₹99/month or ₹999/month, you control your monetization strategy.

6. Easy to Get Started

Creating a channel is free and takes just minutes. No technical skills required—just sign up and start sharing.

How It Works: 5 Simple Steps to Success

Creating and monetizing your channel is incredibly simple. Follow this proven 5-step process from start to earning:

Step 1: Create Channel
Sign up & start building

  1. Create an Account: Sign up for a free account on the platform
  2. Navigate to Channels: Once logged in, go to your dashboard and click on "Channels" or "My Channels"
  3. Click "Create Channel": You'll see a prominent button to create your first channel
  4. Choose a Template: Select from multiple professional templates:
    • Minimalist Template: Clean, simple design perfect for any content type
    • Tech/SaaS Template: Modern design ideal for software and technical content
    • Education/Course Template: Optimized for educational content and courses
    • Creative/Portfolio Template: Beautiful design for creative professionals
    • Business/Professional Template: Corporate look for business content
  5. Set Up Basic Information:
    • Channel Name: Choose a memorable name that reflects your content (e.g., "Web Development Mastery" or "Digital Marketing Pro")
    • Description: Write a compelling description that tells visitors what they'll get
    • Welcome Message: Add a personalized message for new visitors
    • Category: Select the category that best fits your content
Pro Tip: Choose a template that matches your content style. You can always customize colors and branding later!

Step 2: Add Product
Upload your digital file

Now that your channel is created, it's time to add your first piece of content:

  1. Go to Your Channel Dashboard
    • Navigate to your channel
    • Click on "Products" or "Content" tab
  2. Click "Add Product" or "Upload Content"
    • You'll see a button to add new content
  3. Fill in Product Details
    • Title: Clear, descriptive title
    • Description: Detailed description of what subscribers get
    • Type: Select the content type (Video, PDF, Course, Software, Code, Template, etc.)
    • Price: Set price (or mark as free)
    • Tags: Add relevant tags for discoverability
  4. Upload Your File
    • Click the upload area or drag and drop your file
    • Supported formats:
      • PDFs: .pdf
      • Videos: .mp4, .mov, .avi
      • Documents: .doc, .docx
      • Archives: .zip, .rar
      • Images: .jpg, .png, .gif
      • And more!
  5. Set Access Control
    • Subscriber Only: Only subscribers can access
    • Free: Available to everyone
    • Individual Purchase: Can be bought separately
Pro Tip: Start with at least 3-5 pieces of content to give subscribers value from day one!

Step 3: Customize
Design your page

Make your channel uniquely yours with professional branding:

  1. Customize Your Branding
    • Profile Image: Upload a logo or profile picture (recommended: 400x400px)
    • Cover Image: Add a banner image that represents your channel (recommended: 1920x1080px)
    • Color Scheme: Choose primary and secondary colors that match your brand
    • Button Colors: Customize button colors for calls-to-action
  2. Design Your Channel Layout
    • Organize your content sections
    • Set up your welcome page
    • Configure navigation and menus
    • Add featured content

Design Tips:

  • Use high-quality images for a professional look
  • Choose colors that are easy to read and match your brand
  • Ensure your logo is clear and recognizable
  • Keep your cover image simple and not cluttered
  • Make sure your channel reflects your brand identity

Step 4: Publish
Go live instantly

Once you're happy with your setup:

  1. Review All Settings
    • Check your channel information
    • Verify your content is uploaded correctly
    • Ensure branding looks professional
    • Test your channel URL
  2. Click "Publish"

Setting Up Subscription Monetization

Now that your channel is created, let's set up subscriptions so you can start earning.

Step 1: Enable Subscriptions

  1. Go to your channel dashboard
  2. Navigate to the "Subscription" or "Monetization" tab
  3. Toggle "Enable Subscription" to ON

Step 2: Link Your Bank Account

To receive payments, you need to link your bank account:

  1. Click "Link Bank Account" in the subscription settings
  2. Fill in your bank details:
    • Account Holder Name (as it appears on your bank account)
    • Bank Account Number (9-18 digits, no spaces)
    • IFSC Code (format: XXXX0XXXXX)
    • Contact Email (for payment notifications)
    • Contact Phone (with country code)
  3. Confirm that your details are correct
  4. Click "Link Account"
Security Note: Your bank details are encrypted and secure. The platform uses industry-standard security measures to protect your information.

Step 3: Set Your Subscription Price

Choose your monthly subscription price:

  • Consider Your Content Value: What's your content worth? Research similar channels
  • Start Competitive: For new channels, consider starting with a lower price to attract subscribers
  • Currency: Set your price in your preferred currency (INR, USD, etc.)
Pricing Tips:
  • ₹99-₹299/month: Great for beginners or niche content
  • ₹299-₹599/month: Standard for established creators
  • ₹599-₹999/month: Premium content or exclusive access
  • ₹999+/month: High-value content or expert-level courses

Step 4: Understand the Commission Structure

  • You Receive: 85% of subscription payments
  • Platform Fee: 15% commission
  • Payments: Transferred directly to your linked bank account

Example: If someone subscribes for ₹499/month:

  • You receive: ₹424.15 (85%)
  • Platform fee: ₹74.85 (15%)

Step 5: Activate Subscriptions

Once your bank account is linked and price is set:

  1. Review your subscription settings
  2. Ensure everything is correct
  3. Your channel is now ready to accept subscribers!

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: Is it free to create a channel?
A: Yes! Creating a channel is completely free. You only pay a commission (15%) when you receive subscription payments.
Q2: How do I receive payments?
A: Payments are automatically transferred to your linked bank account. You'll receive 85% of each subscription payment.
Q3: Can I change my subscription price later?
A: Yes, you can update your subscription price at any time. Existing subscribers typically keep their original price, while new subscribers pay the updated price.
Q4: What happens if I don't link a bank account?
A: You can create and customize your channel, but you won't be able to enable subscriptions or receive payments until you link your bank account.
Q5: Can I offer both subscriptions and individual product sales?
A: Yes! You can enable subscriptions for access to all content, and also offer individual products for one-time purchase.
Q6: How do I know if my bank account is linked correctly?
A: After linking, you'll see a confirmation message and your account status will show as "Active" in your subscription settings.
Q7: What file sizes can I upload?
A: The platform supports large file uploads. Specific limits may vary, but you can upload videos, PDFs, and other content types without major restrictions.
Q8: Can I make some content free and some paid?
A: Yes! You can set individual content items as free (available to everyone) or subscriber-only (requires subscription).
Q9: How do subscribers access my content?
A: Once someone subscribes, they get instant access to all subscriber-only content. They can view, download, or stream content directly from your channel.
Q10: Can I have multiple channels?
A: Yes, you can create multiple channels for different topics or audiences. Each channel operates independently.
Q11: Is my content secure?
A: Yes, the platform uses industry-standard security measures. Your content and subscriber data are protected with encryption and secure storage.
Q12: How do I cancel or pause subscriptions?
A: You can disable subscriptions at any time from your channel settings. Existing subscribers will retain access until their subscription period ends.
Q13: Can I see who my subscribers are?
A: Yes, you have access to subscriber analytics and can see subscriber count, growth trends, and other metrics in your dashboard.
Q14: What if I need help setting up my channel?
A: The platform provides support documentation, and you can contact support if you need assistance with setup or have questions.
Q15: How long does it take to set up a channel?
A: You can create and publish a basic channel in just 5-10 minutes. Customization and content uploads can take additional time depending on how much you want to customize.
