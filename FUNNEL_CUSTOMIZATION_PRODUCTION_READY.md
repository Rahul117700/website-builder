### 🎯 **How to Use:**

1. **Navigate to Funnel Customization:**
   ```
   Dashboard → My Funnels → Select Funnel → Customize
   ```

2. **Customize Your Funnel:**
   - **Design Tab:** Choose colors, fonts, and upload cover image
   - **Content Tab:** Write headline, subheadline, and CTA
   - **Seller Info Tab:** Add your contact information
   - **Product Tab:** Add product details and pricing

3. **Preview Your Changes:**
   - View in Desktop, Tablet, or Mobile mode
   - See changes in real-time
   - Toggle preview on/off

4. **Save & Publish:**
   - Click "Save" to save as draft
   - Click "Publish" to make it live
   - Status updates in header
