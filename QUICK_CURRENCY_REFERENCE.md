## Pricing Multiplier Quick Guide

**Base: India (INR) = 1.0x**

| Market Category | Multiplier | Example Markets |
|----------------|------------|-----------------|
| **Budget** | 1.0x | India, Philippines, Vietnam, Indonesia |
| **Emerging** | 1.5x - 2x | Brazil, Mexico, Thailand, Malaysia |
| **Growing** | 2x - 3x | South Africa, Turkey, Poland |
| **Developed** | 3x - 4x | Singapore, UAE, Australia |
| **Premium** | 4x - 5x | USA, UK, EU, Canada |

## Quick Calculation Formula

```
Target Price = (India Base Price × Multiplier)
Adjusted for local purchasing power
```

**Example:**
- Base (India): ₹199
- USA Market: ₹199 × 4.5 = ₹896 ≈ **$9** (after local adjustment)

## Regional Pricing Strategies

### 🇮🇳 India Strategy
**Price Point**: ₹199 - ₹999
**Psychology**: Use "9" endings (₹199, ₹499)
**Focus**: Volume over margin
**Best Duration**: 90 days (quarterly)

### 🇺🇸 USA Strategy  
**Price Point**: $9 - $79
**Psychology**: Emphasize ROI and time-saving
**Focus**: Premium positioning
**Best Duration**: Monthly or Annual

### 🇪🇺 Europe Strategy
**Price Point**: €9 - €69
**Psychology**: Show comparisons to competitors
**Focus**: Quality and support
**Best Duration**: Quarterly or Semi-annual

### 🌏 Southeast Asia Strategy
**Price Point**: Similar to or slightly above India
**Psychology**: Local currency = trust
**Focus**: Mobile-first, easy entry
**Best Duration**: Monthly (lower commitment)
