# Home Page - Direct Payment Banner

## ✨ Overview
Added a prominent, eye-catching payment information section to the home page that highlights the direct payment benefit to attract and convert visitors.

---

## 📍 Location
Positioned strategically **between the Hero Section and Features Section** for maximum visibility - visitors see it immediately after the main headline.

---

## 🎨 Design Features

### **Visual Elements**

1. **Gradient Background**
   - Emerald → Teal → Cyan gradient
   - Vibrant, attention-grabbing colors
   - Rounded corners for modern look

2. **Decorative Elements**
   - Floating circles (white opacity 10%)
   - Blur effects for depth
   - Creates premium, professional feel

3. **Badge at Top**
   - "⚡ 100% DIRECT PAYMENTS - ZERO MIDDLEMAN"
   - White text on frosted glass background
   - Eye-catching lightning bolt

---

## 💬 Content Structure

### **Main Headline**
```
💰 Get ALL Your Money Directly to Your Bank!
```
- Large, bold text (2xl → 5xl responsive)
- Underlined "Directly" with yellow decoration
- Money emoji for visual impact

### **Subheadline**
```
Every rupee you earn goes straight to YOUR bank account.
Just connect your Razorpay - No middleman, No delays, No hidden fees!
```
- Highlights "YOUR bank account" in yellow
- Clear, simple language
- Addresses all concerns upfront

## 🚀 "How It Works" Section

```
1. Connect your Razorpay account (takes 2 minutes)
2. Create and publish your products
3. When customer buys, money goes directly to YOUR bank
4. We never hold or touch your money!
```

**Design:**
- Numbered steps (purple)
- Rocket icon
- Easy to scan format
- Step 3 highlighted in green (money flow)
- Step 4 underlined for emphasis

## 🎨 Color Scheme

### Primary Colors
- **Background:** Emerald-500 → Teal-500 → Cyan-500
- **Text:** White with various opacity (95%, 80%)
- **Accents:** Yellow-300, Yellow-400
- **Highlights:** Green-600 (money), Purple-600 (steps)

### Feature Cards
- **Background:** White 20% opacity
- **Backdrop:** Blur effect (frosted glass)
- **Icons:** White in semi-transparent circles

### How It Works Box
- **Background:** White 95% opacity
- **Shadow:** Extra large for prominence
- **Text:** Gray-900 (dark) for readability
