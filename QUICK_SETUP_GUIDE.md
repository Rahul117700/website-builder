# ⚡ Quick Setup Guide - Get Started in 10 Minutes

## 🎯 Your Mission: Create & Publish Your First Funnel

Follow these 4 simple steps to start selling today!

---

## Step 1: Configure Payment Gateway (5 minutes) 💳

### Why? 
You need this to accept payments from customers. Without it, you **cannot publish** your funnel.

### How?

1. **Get Razorpay Account:**
   - Go to: https://dashboard.razorpay.com/signup
   - Sign up (it's free!)
   - Click "Generate Test Keys" in Settings → API Keys

2. **Add to Platform:**
   - Go to **Settings** in your dashboard
   - Click **Payment Gateway** tab
   - Paste your:
     - **Key ID** (starts with `rzp_test_`)
     - **Key Secret** (the longer one)
   - Click **Save Configuration**

✅ **Done!** You'll see a green checkmark.

---

## Step 2: Create Your Funnel (2 minutes) 🎨

1. Click **My Funnels** → **Create New Funnel**
2. Choose template:
   - Software? → Software Funnel
   - Video Course? → Video Sales Funnel  
   - eBook? → Document Sales
3. Enter name and click **Create**

✅ **Done!** You're now in the customizer.

---

## Step 3: Add Your Product (2 minutes) 📦

1. Click **Product** tab (left side)
2. Fill in:
   ```
   Name: Your Product Name
   Description: What's included
   Price: 999 (or your price)
   Type: Choose appropriate type
   ```
3. Click **Choose File** → Select your product
4. Click **Upload Product**
5. Wait for upload to complete

✅ **Done!** Your product is uploaded.

---

## Step 4: Customize & Publish (1 minute) 🚀

### Quick Customization:

**Design Tab:**
- Pick a color preset (Purple/Blue/Green/Red)
- Choose a font

**Content Tab:**
- Edit headline (main selling point)
- Add 4-5 key features

**Seller Info Tab:**
- Add your name and email

### Publish:

1. Click **Save** (top right)
2. Click **Preview** to check
3. Click **Publish** 🎉

✅ **Done!** Your funnel is live!

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Don't:**
- Forget to configure Razorpay (you can't publish!)
- Upload huge files (compress if >50MB)
- Set price to 0
- Skip seller info
- Publish without testing

✅ **Do:**
- Test everything before sharing
- Use clear product images
- Write compelling headlines
- Respond to customers fast
- Keep product files accessible

## 🆘 Getting Stuck?

### Error: "Cannot publish - Payment gateway not configured"

**Fix:** Go to Settings → Payment Gateway → Add Razorpay keys

### Error: "Product upload failed"

**Fix:** 
- Check file size (under 50MB)
- Use supported format (.zip, .pdf, .mp4)
- Try again with better internet

### Error: "Invalid email"

**Fix:** Check email format (needs @)
