## 💡 Pricing Strategy Tips

### 1. **Tier-Based Pricing**
Create 3 tiers for each region:

**Example for India:**
- **Starter**: ₹199/month (60 days) - For beginners
- **Professional**: ₹499/month (90 days) - For serious sellers
- **Business**: ₹999/month (180 days) - For established businesses

**Example for USA:**
- **Starter**: $7/month
- **Professional**: $15/month
- **Business**: $30/month

### 2. **Longer Duration = Better Value**
- 30 days: Base price
- 60 days: 10% discount
- 90 days: 15% discount
- 180 days: 25% discount
- 365 days: 35% discount

**Example:**
- 30 days: ₹199 (₹199/month)
- 90 days: ₹499 (₹166/month) - 16% savings
- 180 days: ₹899 (₹149/month) - 25% savings

Based on ₹199 INR as base (1x):

| Market Tier | Countries | Multiplier | Example |

## 📈 Pricing Psychology

### **Sweet Spot Prices** (Most Conversions)
- India: ₹199, ₹299, ₹499
- USA: $9, $14, $29
- Europe: €9, €14, €29
- UK: £7, £12, £24

### **Avoid These Prices**
- Round numbers (₹200, $10) - seem arbitrary
- Odd numbers (₹173, $11.37) - look random
- Too high for entry (₹999 for first tier)

### **Best Practices**
- Use "9" endings (₹199, $29) - proven to convert better
- Show savings percentage for longer durations
- Display "Most Popular" tag on middle tier
- Offer money-back guarantee to reduce friction

## 🔧 How to Implement Multi-Currency Pricing

### **Option 1: Fixed Exchange Rate (Simple)**
Set prices manually for each currency based on your target profit margin.

**Pros:**
- Full control over pricing
- Can optimize for local markets
- Simple to understand

**Cons:**
- Need to update manually
- Exchange rate fluctuations

### **Option 2: Dynamic Conversion (Advanced)**
Auto-convert from base currency (INR) using live exchange rates.

**Pros:**
- Always up-to-date
- Less maintenance
- Consistent margins

**Cons:**
- Prices change frequently
- May confuse customers
- Less market optimization

### **Recommended: Hybrid Approach**
1. Set base prices in INR
2. Define multipliers for each region (see table above)
3. Update quarterly based on:
   - Exchange rates
   - Market feedback
   - Competition
   - Sales data

## 💰 Revenue Optimization Tips

### **1. A/B Test Pricing**
- Test ±20% price variations
- Track conversion rates
- Optimize for revenue, not just conversions

### **2. Seasonal Pricing**
- Launch discounts: 30% off first month
- Festival offers: Diwali (India), Black Friday (US)
- New Year promotions: 40-50% off

### **3. Bundle Discounts**
- Buy 6 months, get 2 months free
- Annual plans: 3 months free equivalent
- Referral credits: ₹100 off per referral

### **4. Volume Discounts for Agencies**
- 5+ users: 20% off
- 10+ users: 35% off
- Custom enterprise: Contact sales

### **5. Student/Nonprofit Discounts**
- 50% off with valid ID
- Great for building brand loyalty
- Creates future paying customers
